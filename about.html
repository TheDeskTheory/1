Who we are

A small print shop for people who still like holding paper.

Loose Leaf Press started as a class project and a shared love of print — the kind of posters you'd pin above a desk, bookmarks that make you want to finish the book, and stickers that survive a water bottle's entire life cycle. We design everything ourselves, in small runs, with a duotone riso-style palette that's become our signature.

Why paper goods

Low cost to produce, easy to ship, and genuinely useful — the kind of product a small team can actually run end to end.

Why the riso look

Layered, slightly imperfect color feels handmade in a way that flat digital prints don't.

Where we're headed

More formats — postcards and notebooks are next — and a stronger focus on local, uncrowded design themes.
