Who we are

Art for every corner — one shelf, one page, one wall at a time.

The Desk Theory started as a class project and a shared love of small, considered objects — the kind of poster you'd pin above a desk, a bookmark that makes you want to finish the book, a sticker that survives years on a water bottle. We design everything ourselves, in small runs, with an earthy, hand-drawn palette that's become our signature.

Why paper goods

Low cost to produce, easy to ship, and genuinely useful — the kind of product a small team can actually run end to end.

Why this palette

Deep charcoal, brick red, and moss green feel handmade and a little worn-in — like a well-loved notebook, not a showroom.

Where we're headed

More formats — postcards and notebooks are next — and a stronger focus on local, uncrowded design themes.
